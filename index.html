About

Director Profile

With 8 years of working on films and TV commercials, Raghu Vardhan has come a long way as a filmmaker and visual storyteller.

He worked as an Associate Director on the critically acclaimed GHAZI ATTACK, being involved from research through final production and serving as a major support to director Mr. Sankalp Reddy.

He was also part of two Telugu independent films, including SHIKARAM, which received the Gold Nandi in 2014.
